CameraWatcher

An Android app that turns an old phone into a motion-activated security camera.

What it does

  • Watches the camera image and records a video clip only when something moves.
  • Stamps date and time on the video and builds a short daily timelapse.
  • Keeps clips on the phone and, if you sign in with Google, uploads them to your own Google Drive.

Google Drive access

The app asks for one permission, drive.file. It lets the app see and manage only the files and folders the app itself created in your Drive (a folder named “Камера видеонаблюдения” with clips sorted by date). It cannot read any of your other files. Nothing is sent to any server run by the developer.

Privacy

No ads, no analytics, no accounts on our side. Read the Privacy Policy.
