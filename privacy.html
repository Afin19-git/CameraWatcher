CameraWatcher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who we are

CameraWatcher is a personal Android app that records video when motion is detected. It is developed by an individual developer. Contact: a8189734@gmail.com

What data the app handles

  • Camera and microphone. Video and audio are recorded on your phone only while motion is detected (audio can be turned off). Recordings are stored in the app’s folder on the phone.
  • Google account. If you choose to sign in, the app uses Google Play Services to get permission to your Google Drive. The app sees only your account’s access token, which Google manages. We never see your password.
  • Google Drive files. The app requests the drive.file scope. With it the app can create, upload, list and delete only the files and folders that the app itself created. It cannot read your other Drive files.

How the data is used

Recorded clips and timelapse videos are uploaded to a folder in your own Google Drive so you can watch them later. The app deletes its own old files from your Drive and phone according to the retention periods you set in the app. Data is used for nothing else.

Sharing and storage

The developer has no server and receives none of your data. We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and we use no analytics or advertising. Recordings live only on your phone and in your own Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CameraWatcher’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • Sign out of Google Drive in the app’s Settings at any time.
  • Revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete recordings from the phone or from your Drive, or uninstall the app to remove local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)
